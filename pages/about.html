Our story

KIDSE TOYS started with a simple obsession: those viral squishy and plush toys that keep taking over everyone's feeds. We were tired of seeing them everywhere online but never being able to actually get our hands on the good ones — the ones with the perfect slow-rise squish and the softest plush.

So we built a shop dedicated entirely to the best of them. Every character in our lineup is hand-picked for maximum squish, cuddle factor, and collect-them-all charm. From $5 minis to giant collector bundles, there's something here for every fidgeter, gifter, and super-fan.
